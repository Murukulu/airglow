# 2026-07-19

Today, @saiputravu and @tcassar decided on a model to implement. 

Started by discussing:
- Nowcasting
- Short-range forecasting
- Medium-range forecasting
- Long-range forecasting

Remarked that nowcasting is effectively a solved problem - what value would we be 
adding over BBC weather?
A more interesting place to be is in _medium-range_ forcasting. 

This is the kind of range where having precise data can genuinely help preparations.
If you learn a storm is coming half an hour before it hits, you are limited in the 
genuine prep you can make. Three days forewarning, however, can be enough to make a 
difference.

Thus, we reviewed three options:
- Aurora
  - **Not** SOTA
  - Still used in literature as a relevant indicator of model performance
  - Open source weights, architecutre make our lives easier (implementation-wise)
  - Skin in the game: researches releasing research typically good enough
- AIFS
  - Is SOTA
  - But, requires a _lot_ of paper reading
  - Architecutre is more complex than that of Aurora
  - Size of model currently unknown (this has an impact on how computationally easy 
    inference will be)
- NVIDIA Earth models
  - "Skin in the game" concerns: NVIDIA want to show their chips allow cool AI things
    to happen. This isn't _really_ motivation to be at the cutting edge of weather 
    models. Better off let other people use your hardware and figure out the difficult
    stuff themselves
  - No compelling reason (from literature) to take over AIFS/Aurora, except their 
    models span all forecasting timelines - not currently something we are looking to 
    implement

EPT-2(e) is a SOTA model run by a company who sell their weather model to infra/hedge
funds. Hence, Jua (the parent company) has no incentive to make their open source model
their best offering.

Thus, we selected **AIFS** to implement despite it's increased difficulty and our 
limited time budget. The thinking is:
  - Three weeks to push AIFS
  - Lots of our learning in the meantime _will be transferrable_ if we fall back to 
    Aurora, even though the model arch itself won't be
  - AIFS is shinier and more exciting: might as well _try_ something more interesting.

If, after three weeks, we decide that AIFS is not feasable, we agreed to fall back to 
Aurora.
